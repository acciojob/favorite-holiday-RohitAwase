Goa


Goa is one of the most popular holiday destinations in India. It is famous for its beautiful beaches, vibrant nightlife, Portuguese architecture, delicious seafood, and relaxing atmosphere.

Top 5 Attractions

  1. Baga Beach - Famous for water sports and nightlife.
  2. Calangute Beach - Known as the "Queen of Beaches".
  3. Fort Aguada - A historic Portuguese fort.
  4. Basilica of Bom Jesus - A UNESCO World Heritage Site.
  5. Dudhsagar Waterfalls - One of India's tallest waterfalls.

Must-Try Foods

  • Goan Fish Curry – Best enjoyed at Ritz Classic, Panaji
  • Prawn Balchão – Try at Mum's Kitchen, Panaji
  • Chicken Xacuti – Available at Martin's Corner, Betalbatim
  • Bebinca (Traditional Goan dessert) – Try at Confeitaria 31 de Janeiro, Panaji
  • Vindaloo – Famous at The Fisherman's Wharf, Cavelossim
